# Choosing My Future Employer: Google
A Student Career & Company Research Presentation

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the research notes, structure, and documentation for a professional presentation analyzing Google as a prospective future employer for IT and Software Engineering students. 

The presentation evaluates Google’s global standing, corporate culture, core technologies, and specific entry-level career opportunities, structured into an engaging, visual Prezi narrative.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

    Company Profiling: Provide an in-depth overview of Google’s mission, global scale, and engineering values.
    Tech Stack & Scale: Highlight core engineering practices, distributed systems, open-source contributions, and AI/ML innovations.
    Career Path Analysis: Break down entry-level roles (e.g., Junior Software Engineer - L3), growth ladders, mentorship, and work-life balance.
    Interview & Preparation Guide: Outline practical preparation steps (data structures, algorithms, system design, and behavioral interviews).



---

## 🗂 Presentation Structure

The presentation is organized into 5 core thematic sections (12 slides):

1. Introduction

    Slide 1: Title & Motivation — Choosing My Future Employer: Google


2. Company Profile

    Slide 2: Company Overview (Global reach, business model, scale)
    Slide 3: Why Google? (Culture of innovation, psychological safety, perks & learning)


3. Tech & Projects

    Slide 4: Engineering at Scale (Global infrastructure & reliability)
    Slide 5: The Tech Stack (Languages, frameworks, distributed platforms like Borg/Kubernetes)
    Slide 6: Key Projects & Open Source (Chromium, Android, TensorFlow, Gemini)


4. Career Path & Opportunities

    Slide 7: Career Path & Opportunities Overview
    Slide 8: Junior Software Engineer (L3) Role & Responsibilities
    Slide 9: Engineering Career Ladder (L3 to L8+) & Mentorship
    Slide 10: Market Position & Competitor Benchmark
    Slide 11: Preparation & Hiring Process (LeetCode, DSA, Googleyness)


5. Conclusion

    Slide 12: Summary, Q&A, and Closing Remarks
